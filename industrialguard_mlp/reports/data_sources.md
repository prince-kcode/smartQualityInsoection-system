## Considered but not downloaded

- [Amazon Kaputt](https://huggingface.co/datasets/amazon/kaputt) is a much larger broad-product anomaly resource (about 328 GB) behind Hugging Face access and terms acceptance. It was not downloaded because access is gated and the footprint is disproportionate for this desktop project; the available MVTec, VisA, MVTec AD 2, and bottle datasets are already integrated. Its license/terms also require separate review before redistribution or commercial use.

## Extraction and labeling notes

- MVTec AD: 15 category folders. `train/good` images train the category MLP; category test folders preserve normal/defect tags for held-out screening evaluation.
- VisA: categories and image-level labels are read from `split_csv/1cls.csv`; labels are not inferred from filenames.
- MVTec AD 2: only public data is used. `train/good` trains the category head, `validation/good` calibrates category uncertainty, and `test_public/good|bad` is used for held-out evaluation.
- NEU-CLS: the six labels are derived from the documented filename prefixes Cr, In, Pa, PS, RS, and Sc. An 80/20 stratified train/test split with a held-out validation portion is deterministic (seed 73).
- The MVTec AD, VisA, MVTec AD 2, PharmaVision, and ETID category images share one product-classifier label space after the VisA spelling `capsules` is normalized to `capsule`; NEU-CLS remains a separate task.
- ETID's source groups are held out globally so photos from a capture group cannot cross training, validation, and test. The ETID test split measures product-category recognition only. Its photos are excluded from defect evaluation and from all normal-reference banks because no defect/normal labels are supplied.
- PharmaVision normal and defective examples share the existing `pill` product label, improving category recognition across tablet condition variation. Its test images stay held out. The paper reports aggregate severity counts, but the downloadable archive has no image-level severity map; no minor/major head is trained from it.
- The bottle dataset's YOLO class names pair five defect types with `marginal`/`unacceptable` quality grades. Its official image splits also feed a separate `plastic_shampoo_bottle` product label; all these source images stay out of normal-reference features. The bottle detector is trained on the official train split, model selection uses validation, and the official test split is used once for final metrics. “Marginal” is shown as a minor-like clue and “unacceptable” as a major-like clue; neither label mapping is used for other product categories.
- MVTec AD's annotated fault-folder names are mapped into broad defect types to train a localized-region classifier head. These derived labels remain limited to MVTec-like defect crops and are not a universal defect taxonomy or severity label.
- MVTec AD and MVTec AD 2 are explicitly non-commercial datasets; this student demo should remain within the license terms unless the rights holders grant additional permission.
